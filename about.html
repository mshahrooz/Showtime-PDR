About Showtime PDR

Paintless dent repair in Bellingham, Washington. We help you find out what can be repaired while keeping the finish your vehicle came with.

Our Story

Good Work Starts With an Honest Assessment

Showtime Paintless Dent Repair helps Bellingham drivers deal with dents and dings without refinishing a panel when the original paint can be saved.

From a parking-lot ding to a crease along a body line, the first step is to look at the shape of the dent, the condition of the paint, and whether the metal can be reached.

When paintless dent repair is a good fit, the panel is reshaped in small, controlled movements. The factory finish stays in place without sanding, body filler, or a new coat of paint.

Some damage needs a different approach. We explain what’s possible before you decide how to move forward.

The Experience

Guided From Start to Finish

Start with a close-up, an angled photo, and a wider view of the panel. Tell us what happened and whether you can see any chips or cracks in the paint.

We’ll look at the photos, explain whether PDR is worth exploring, and let you know if an in-person inspection would help. When the repair can go ahead, we’ll talk you through the next steps.

What you can expect: a straightforward estimate, a clear explanation of what can be repaired, and careful work that prioritizes the original finish.
